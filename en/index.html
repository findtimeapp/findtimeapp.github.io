Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account in the app. Your profile, profile photo, friendships, group memberships and the events you created are deleted right away. If you can no longer sign in, write to us from the email address the account uses and we will delete it for you.

What does Find Time see in my calendar?

Only when you are busy – start and end times. Titles, locations, attendees and notes are never read or sent anywhere. Read more in our privacy policy.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the person's name or photo and choose Report or Block. Groups and events can be reported with the button at the bottom of their page. We review every report within 24 hours. People you have blocked are listed under Settings → Blocked users.

I forgot my password

Write to us from the email address you use in Find Time and we will help you.

I did not get a confirmation email

Check your spam folder. The email comes from findtime.support@gmail.com. If it has not arrived after a few minutes, write to us.
