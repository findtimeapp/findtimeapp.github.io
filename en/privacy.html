Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy explains what the Find Time app stores about you, why, who can see it and what rights you have.

1. Data controller

Bjørn Mejneche Larsen, private individual, Denmark
Email: findtime.support@gmail.com

2. What we store

  • Account: your email address and an encrypted version of your password. If you sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from them – never your password.
  • Profile: name, username, profile colour and a profile photo if you choose to add one. The photo is stored in low resolution (128 × 128 pixels).
  • Friends and groups: who you are friends with, friend requests and the groups you belong to.
  • Events: title, emoji, the period being planned, invitations, suggested days, your votes and the day that is chosen.
  • Busy times: if you give access to your calendar, the app reads only the start and end times within an event's period. Titles, locations, attendees and notes are never read or sent. You can also mark busy days by hand.
  • Safety: who you have blocked, and reports you send or that are sent about you, including the reason and text written in the report.
  • Technical: your sign-in session is stored on your phone so you stay signed in. Our provider keeps short technical logs (such as IP address and time) to keep the service secure.

Find Time has no ads, no tracking across apps and websites and no analytics tools. We never sell your data.

3. Who can see what

  • Other users can find you by your name or username and see your name, username, profile colour and profile photo.
  • The participants of an event can see who is invited, the busy times you have shared within the event's period, suggestions and votes.
  • The members of a group can see each other and the group's events.
  • Who you have blocked and what you have reported is visible only to you and us.

4. Purposes and legal basis

  • To provide the app – create your account, show you your friends, groups and events and find a day that works – we process data to perform our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep Find Time safe – handle reports, prevent abuse and remove offensive content – we process data based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase runs our database, sign-in and file storage. Data is stored on servers in Ireland (EU).
  • Google (Gmail) is used to send emails from the app and to answer support requests.
  • Apple, Google and Facebook (Meta) only receive data if you choose to sign in with them. They handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies.

Supabase and Google are US companies. If data is processed outside the EU, this is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data until you delete your account. When you delete it under Settings → Delete account, your profile, profile photo, friendships, blocks, group memberships, busy times and votes and the events you created are deleted. Reports you sent are kept without your name so we can close the case. Reports about you are deleted. Technical logs are deleted automatically after a short time.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you and to have it corrected, deleted or handed over in a machine-readable format. You can also object to or ask us to restrict processing. Write to findtime.support@gmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk, or to the authority in your own country.

8. Age

You must be at least 13 years old to use Find Time.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For significant changes we let you know in the app.
